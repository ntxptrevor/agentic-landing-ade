Topic and date come from the caller. Write knowledge/research/<date>-<topic>.md: typical sequence differences vs the NTXP backbone, durations ranges, regulatory hold points (Texas agencies: TDLR, DSHS, HHSC, TCJS, TCEQ, fire marshal), procurement-method effects (JOC, CSP) on preconstruction, with source links.
End with `## Proposals` (may be empty): each one a concrete change to a module or backbone section with the sources. Never include project, owner or pricing data. Never edit data files.
